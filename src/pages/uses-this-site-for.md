## What I use this site for

This is my simple public notebook, portfolio, and static publishing space. I write in Markdown and keep the build lightweight so content stays easy to export and control.

## Primary purposes

- Personal homepage & contact: links to my work and how to reach me.
- Notes & essays: longer posts and quick ideas.
- Project showcase: short project pages with links to repos or demos.
- Technical reference: commands, bookmarks, and short guides I reuse.

## What you'll find

- Markdown articles and short posts.
- A `/uses` page with tools and hardware/software notes.
- Bookmarks and useful links.
- Project pages and brief changelog entries.
